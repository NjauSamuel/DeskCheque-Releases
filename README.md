# DeskCheque

Read M-Pesa / PayBill payment confirmation SMS on your Windows PC from a USB GSM modem, with a quick popup for copying payment amounts.

## Download

[![Download v0.1.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_v0.1.0-Windows-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge&logo=windows)](https://github.com/OWNER/DeskCheque-Releases/releases/latest)

Click the badge to download the installer directly. Run the `.exe` and follow the prompts.

## How to install

1. Click Download above.
2. Run the `.exe` and follow the on-screen prompts.
3. DeskCheque starts and appears in your system tray (near the clock).
4. Plug in your USB GSM modem with an active SIM, then open DeskCheque and select the modem COM port in Settings.

## What it does

- SMS listening — reads incoming payment confirmation SMS from a USB modem SIM
- Quick panel — press F5 for a popup of recent payments; copy amounts in one click
- Auto-updates — checks on startup and every 6 hours, then installs and relaunches when a new version is ready

## System requirements

- Windows 10 (1809+) or 11 (64-bit)
- A USB GSM modem with an active SIM that receives the SMS
- WebView2 Runtime (preinstalled on updated Windows 10/11)

## Troubleshooting

**The app doesn't open** — Check the system tray near the clock; right-click the tray icon to open it.

**No SMS appear** — Confirm the modem COM port is selected in Settings and the SIM has signal.

**Need to share logs** — Open DeskCheque Settings and share the log file with support.
